## Empowering 2,000+ Kenyan Suppliers to Thrive in the Digital Procurement Era

Kenya’s transition to the **eGP (Electronic Government Procurement)** system marks a bold leap toward transparency, efficiency, and accountability in public procurement. But for many suppliers, this shift can feel overwhelming. That’s where we come in.

I offer a transformative training and onboarding experience designed to equip suppliers with the skills, confidence, and systems mastery needed to succeed on eGP—from registration to bidding and beyond.

\---

### 💡 Why This Training Matters

* **eGP is now mandatory** for all government tenders—non-compliance means missed opportunities.
* Many suppliers lack the digital literacy or workflow clarity to navigate the platform effectively.
* Our training bridges the gap with **hands-on guidance**, **modular systems**, and **faith-driven support**.

\---

### 📚 What You’ll Learn

* ✅ Step-by-step onboarding to the eGP portal
* ✅ How to prepare compliant bids and tender documents
* ✅ Navigating dashboards, alerts, and submission workflows
* ✅ Avoiding common pitfalls and disqualifications
* ✅ Strategic positioning for long-term success

\---

### 💼 Who This Is For

* Small and medium enterprises (SMEs)
* First-time government suppliers
* Women- and youth-led businesses
* Faith-based organizations seeking procurement access
* Any supplier ready to scale with confidence
