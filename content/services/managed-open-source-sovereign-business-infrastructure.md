### The Vision
Digital sovereignty is the ultimate competitive advantage. By moving your business operations to managed open-source systems, you eliminate monthly per-user SaaS fees and take complete ownership of your data, your privacy, and your future.

### The Process
*   **Legacy Exit Strategy:** We identify the proprietary SaaS tools slowing you down and map their high-performance open-source replacements.
*   **Automated Provisioning:** Deploying a unified server stack that houses your entire business—from email and chat to project management and ERP.
*   **Hardening & Security:** Implementing enterprise-grade encryption and zero-trust access control across all self-hosted nodes.

### Deliverables
*   **Unified Sovereign Stack:** A single, managed ecosystem containing all your core business tools.
*   **Data Governance Policy:** Clear protocols for how your organization handles and secures its private data.
*   **Ongoing Maintenance Roadmap:** A plan for automated updates and 99.9% uptime reliability.

### Tech Stack Expertise
*   **Core Systems:** Nextcloud, Matrix/Element, Odoo, Bitwarden/Vaultwarden.
*   **Infrastructure:** Linux (Ubuntu/Debian), Docker Compose, Traefik Reverse Proxy.
*   **Backup:** automated off-site encrypted backups to S3-compatible storage.
