### The Vision
Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) are the backbone of the economy, but they often struggle with fragmented, expensive, and outdated technology. I help SMEs leapfrog the competition by deploying unified digital systems that automate workflows and reduce overhead.

### The Process
*   **Operational Inventory:** We map your current business processes to identify where manual effort and legacy software are costing you time and money.
*   **Unified Tooling:** Consolidating your operations into a single, high-performance platform that handles everything from sales and inventory to payroll.
*   **Team Empowerment:** I train your staff to master these new tools, ensuring high adoption rates and long-term success.

### Deliverables
*   **SME Digital Maturity Report:** A clear assessment of your current state and a roadmap for full digitalization.
*   **Integrated Business Platform:** A customized ERP/CRM ecosystem tailored specifically to your industry.
*   **Workflow Automation Suite:** High-impact automations that remove manual bottlenecks in your day-to-day operations.

### Tech Stack Expertise
*   **Business Tools:** Odoo, ERPNext, Frappe.
*   **CRM & Sales:** Hubspot (Integration), Self-Hosted CRM.
*   **Collaboration:** Mattermost, Zulip, Element.
