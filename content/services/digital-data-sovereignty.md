### The Vision
Data is the new gold, but in a proprietary cloud, you are just a tenant on your own land. I help organizations migrate to infrastructures where they—and only they—hold the keys to their data, ensuring 100% privacy and zero vendor lock-in.

### The Process
*   **Sovereignty Audit:** Evaluating your current data footprint to identify where privacy is leaking and where control has been surrendered.
*   **Infrastructure Migration:** Building and deploying private, encrypted data vaults that replace generic public cloud storage.
*   **Key Management Protocols:** Establishing rigorous standards for encryption keys and access control to ensure long-term security.

### Deliverables
*   **Data Sovereignty Blueprint:** A comprehensive plan for complete data ownership.
*   **Self-Hosted Storage Ecosystem:** Fully functional, secure alternatives to Dropbox, Google Drive, and OneDrive.
*   **Privacy Hardening Report:** A detailed breakdown of the encryption and security layers protecting your data.

### Tech Stack Expertise
*   **Sovereign Storage:** Nextcloud, OwnCloud, Seafile.
*   **Encryption:** PGP/GPG, LUKS, VeraCrypt, Vaultwarden.
*   **Privacy:** Tor, I2P, VPN/WireGuard Tunneling, Zero-Knowledge Proofs.
